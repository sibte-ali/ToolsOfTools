## What is the Unicode Text Converter?

The **Unicode Text Converter** transforms standard alphanumeric characters into dozens of unique, stylized typographical variations using characters mapped across the international **Unicode Consortium** character specification.

Instead of generating raster images or requiring special CSS stylesheet downloads, this tool produces pure, copy-pasteable Unicode text that works anywhere on the internet.
