## Understanding Agricultural & Real Estate Land Measures

Land and property acreage are measured in different units depending on region and regulatory standards. While the metric **hectare** ($ha$) is standard in Europe, Australia, India, and scientific forestry, the imperial **acre** remains the legal standard for property listings in the United States and the United Kingdom.
