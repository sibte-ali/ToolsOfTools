## Understanding Exam Marks and Percentage Calculation

Calculating your aggregate marks percentage is an essential milestone following school board evaluations, college midterms, and university degree examinations. Whether you are completing secondary school under CBSE, ICSE, or State Boards, or wrapping up undergraduate degree courses, presenting an exact aggregate percentage is required for college cutoffs, competitive entrance eligibility, and job employment criteria.

While the fundamental concept is straightforward, variations in subject maximums (such as 100-mark theory subjects versus 50-mark practical or elective courses) require systematic calculation.

### Fundamental Percentage Formula

The standard formula to compute academic percentage across single or multiple subjects is:

$$\text{Aggregate Percentage} = \left( \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} M_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} T_i} \right) \times 100$$

Where:
- $M_i$ represents marks obtained in subject $i$
- $T_i$ represents maximum possible marks for subject $i$
- $n$ is the total number of subjects evaluated

If all $n$ subjects carry equal maximum marks of $100$ each:
$$\text{Percentage} = \frac{\sum M_i}{n \times 100} \times 100 = \frac{\sum M_i}{n}$$

### Step-by-Step Worked Demonstration

Consider a student who receives marks across six subjects in the higher secondary board examinations:

| Subject | Marks Obtained ($M_i$) | Maximum Marks ($T_i$) | Subject % |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Core | 88 | 100 | 88.0% |
| Mathematics | 92 | 100 | 92.0% |
| Physics (Theory + Lab) | 84 | 100 | 84.0% |
| Chemistry (Theory + Lab) | 79 | 100 | 79.0% |
| Computer Science | 96 | 100 | 96.0% |
| Physical Education | 90 | 100 | 90.0% |
| **Total Marks** | **529** | **600** | — |

1. **Sum Obtained Marks**: $88 + 92 + 84 + 79 + 96 + 90 = 529$
2. **Sum Maximum Marks**: $100 \times 6 = 600$
3. **Calculate Percentage**:
   $$\text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{529}{600} \right) \times 100 = 88.166...\% \to \mathbf{88.17\%}$$
4. **Division Classification**: With 88.17%, the student achieves **First Division with Distinction** ($\ge 75\%$).

### Academic Divisions and Grading Standards

Most universities and state secondary boards classify student achievements according to well-defined brackets:

- **Distinction (Honors)**: $\ge 75\%$
- **First Class / Division**: $60.0\% - 74.9\%$
- **Second Class / Division**: $50.0\% - 59.9\%$
- **Third Class / Pass**: $33.0\% - 49.9\%$ (or $35\% - 49.9\%$ in select state boards)
- **Fail**: $< 33\%$ in theory or practical components
