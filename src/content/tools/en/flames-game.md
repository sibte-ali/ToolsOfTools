## What is the FLAMES Game?

The **FLAMES game** is an iconic paper-and-pencil relationship game played by generations of students across classrooms and summer camps worldwide. Written in notebooks during study periods, the game provided endless amusement and friendly teasing.

Our online FLAMES calculator preserves the exact rules of the traditional game while showing you the complete round-by-round elimination sequence right in your browser.

### The Six FLAMES Outcomes

The acronym **F-L-A-M-E-S** represents six playful relational categories:

- **F – Friends:** A relaxed, enduring companionship filled with laughs, mutual support, and zero drama.
- **L – Love:** A romantic attraction accompanied by starry-eyed daydreaming and emotional flutter.
- **A – Affection:** A gentle, tender fondness characterized by warm consideration and quiet respect.
- **M – Marriage:** A solid, committed partnership built for long-term adventures, stability, and family life.
- **E – Enemies:** A dramatic, clash-prone dynamic full of stubborn arguments and comedic rivalry.
- **S – Siblings:** A protective, familial bond featuring friendly bickering and unshakeable loyalty.

### Exact Step-by-Step Rules

Here is how the calculation works from start to finish:

1. **Write Down Both Names:** Remove spaces, middle names, and symbols, converting all letters to lowercase.
2. **Strike Out Common Letters:** Compare both names letter by letter. Whenever a letter appears in both names, strike one instance out from each name.
3. **Count the Remainder (N):** Sum the number of unmatched letters left across both names. This number is $N$.
4. **Perform Cyclic Elimination:** Write down the letters `F`, `L`, `A`, `M`, `E`, `S`. Count up to $N$ starting from the first letter. Strike out the letter where the count lands. Continue counting from the next adjacent letter until only one letter remains.

#### Worked Example: "John" and "Mary"

- Name 1: **J - O - H - N** (4 letters)
- Name 2: **M - A - R - Y** (4 letters)
- Common letters: None.
- Total remaining letters $N = 4 + 4 = 8$.

Counting 8 positions through `[F, L, A, M, E, S]`:
- Round 1: Count 8 lands on **B** (second letter after cycling through 6), eliminating **L**. Remaining: `[F, A, M, E, S]`.
- Round 2: Count 8 from **A** lands on **A**, eliminating **A**. Remaining: `[F, M, E, S]`.
- Round 3: Count 8 from **M** lands on **S**, eliminating **S**. Remaining: `[F, M, E]`.
- Round 4: Count 8 from **F** lands on **M**, eliminating **M**. Remaining: `[F, E]`.
- Round 5: Count 8 from **E** lands on **F**, eliminating **F**.
- Final Survivor: **E** (**Enemies**).
