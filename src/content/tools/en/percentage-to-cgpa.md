## Reverse Conversion: Translating Percentage into CGPA

While many students transition from CGPA to percentage, the reverse need arises frequently. When applying to international universities, preparing transcripts for foreign credential evaluation agencies (such as World Education Services / WES), or applying to multinational employers whose recruitment portals enforce a GPA input field, converting your raw percentage into a standardized CGPA is required.

Because Indian academic boards established specific algebraic equations linking grade points to percentages, performing the reverse calculation requires inverting the official statutory multiplier.

### Mathematical Inversion by Educational Board

#### 1. Inverting the CBSE 9.5 Formula
CBSE established that $\text{Percentage} = \text{CGPA} \times 9.5$. To reverse this:
$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\text{Percentage}}{9.5}$$

- A score of **95%** converts to $95 / 9.5 = \mathbf{10.00\text{ CGPA}}$
- A score of **85.5%** converts to $85.5 / 9.5 = \mathbf{9.00\text{ CGPA}}$
- A score of **76%** converts to $76 / 9.5 = \mathbf{8.00\text{ CGPA}}$
- A score of **66.5%** converts to $66.5 / 9.5 = \mathbf{7.00\text{ CGPA}}$

#### 2. Inverting the VTU Engineering Formula
VTU Belagavi defines percentage as $\text{Percentage} = (\text{CGPA} - 0.75) \times 10$. Inverting this equation yields:
$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\text{Percentage}}{10} + 0.75$$

- A score of **72.5%** converts to $(72.5 / 10) + 0.75 = 7.25 + 0.75 = \mathbf{8.00\text{ CGPA}}$
- A score of **65.0%** converts to $(65.0 / 10) + 0.75 = 6.50 + 0.75 = \mathbf{7.25\text{ CGPA}}$

#### 3. Standard 10-Point Scale (Direct Linear Mapping)
For universities adhering to direct linear scaling without an offset (such as Anna University):
$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\text{Percentage}}{10}$$
- A score of **82%** converts directly to $\mathbf{8.20\text{ CGPA}}$.

### Worked Demonstration

Consider a graduate who obtained an aggregate score of 85.5% across secondary board examinations:
1. **Identify the Prescribed Rule**: The issuing board is CBSE.
2. **Apply the Equation**: $\text{CGPA} = 85.5 / 9.5$.
3. **Calculate**: $85.5 / 9.5 = 9.00$.
4. **Classification**: 9.00 CGPA qualifies as "First Class with Distinction" or Outstanding.

### Practical Tips for Job Seekers and Graduate Applicants

- **Check Portal Guidance**: Some job application portals explicitly mandate using the direct factor ($/ 10$) regardless of board affiliation. Always prioritize the employer's specific instructions.
- **Provide Conversion Certificates**: If your university uses an offset (like VTU or Mumbai University), attach an official conversion certificate issued by your registrar when submitting applications to overseas universities.
