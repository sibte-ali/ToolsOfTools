## Desktop Publishing with Shree Lipi and Unicode

**Shree-Lipi** has been the industry standard for regional language desktop publishing (DTP) in India for over three decades, especially for Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati, and Sanskrit books, magazines, and newspapers.

While modern operating systems and web browsers rely exclusively on **Unicode**, print shops and prepress houses continue to maintain extensive archives of Shree-Dev fonts. This converter bridges the gap between digital Unicode copy (from websites, emails, or Google Docs) and print-ready Shree-Dev typography.

### Common Use Cases
1. **Typesetting in Adobe PageMaker:** Older versions of Adobe PageMaker cannot render Unicode Hindi without broken ligatures. Converting to Shree Lipi allows perfect typesetting.
2. **CorelDraw Vector Graphics:** Prepare Marathi and Hindi vector logos, wedding cards, flex boards, and book covers without font rendering bugs.
3. **Newspaper & Magazine Layouts:** Quickly ingest reporter copy typed in Unicode into Shree-Lipi editorial templates.

### Workflow Tips
- Type or compose your article in standard Hindi/Marathi on your computer or phone using Google Input Tools or Gboard.
- Paste the Unicode text into this converter.
- Copy the converted output and paste it directly into your design software (PageMaker, InDesign, or CorelDraw) with your preferred **Shree-Dev** font selected.
