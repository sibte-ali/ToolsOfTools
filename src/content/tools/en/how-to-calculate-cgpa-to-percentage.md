## Understanding CGPA to Percentage Conversion

In modern educational institutions, grade cards predominantly feature Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) rather than percentage marks. However, competitive recruitment drives, civil service examinations (such as UPSC and SSC), state public service commissions, campus placement drives, and postgraduate scholarship applications frequently mandate reporting an exact percentage.

Because different academic boards and universities adopt distinct conversion metrics, applying the correct institutional equation is essential to avoid application disqualification.

### Official Conversion Formulas by University Board

#### 1. CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education)
For CBSE Class 10 and secondary education:
$$\text{Percentage} = \text{CGPA} \times 9.5$$
To calculate subject-wise percentage from individual Grade Points (GP):
$$\text{Subject Percentage} = \text{Subject GP} \times 9.5$$

#### 2. VTU (Visvesvaraya Technological University)
For engineering and technology programs governed by VTU Belagavi:
$$\text{Percentage} = (\text{CGPA} - 0.75) \times 10$$
*Example:* A VTU CGPA of 8.25 converts to $(8.25 - 0.75) \times 10 = 75.0\%$.

#### 3. Mumbai University (10-Point CBCS Scale)
Mumbai University specifies two tiers for undergraduate programs:
- For CGPA less than 7.0: $\text{Percentage} = 7.1 \times \text{CGPA} + 12$
- For CGPA 7.0 and above: $\text{Percentage} = 7.25 \times \text{CGPA} + 11$

#### 4. Standard UGC and Anna University Scale
Under University Grants Commission (UGC) guidelines and Anna University Chennai regulations, colleges that do not prescribe custom offsets apply direct scaling:
$$\text{Percentage} = \text{CGPA} \times 10$$

### Step-by-Step Worked Example

Suppose a student graduates with a CGPA of 8.40 on a standard 10-point scale:
- **Under CBSE Framework**: $8.40 \times 9.5 = 79.80\%$
- **Under VTU Framework**: $(8.40 - 0.75) \times 10 = 7.65 \times 10 = 76.50\%$
- **Under Mumbai University Framework**: $(7.25 \times 8.40) + 11 = 60.90 + 11 = 71.90\%$
- **Under Anna University / UGC Direct**: $8.40 \times 10 = 84.00\%$

Notice the significant variation between 71.90% and 84.00% for the exact same grade point average. Always consult the back of your official semester transcript where your university's statutory conversion formula is printed.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Assuming a Flat Multiplication by 10**: Multiplying an 8.4 CGPA by 10 to claim 84% on a job application where CBSE 9.5 was required constitutes academic misrepresentation.
- **Confusing SGPA with CGPA**: Never convert a single semester's SGPA and report it as your cumulative degree score.
