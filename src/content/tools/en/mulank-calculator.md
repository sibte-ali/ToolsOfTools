## What is a Mulank (Driver Number)?

In the ancient tradition of Indian numerology (*Ank Jyotish*), your **Mulank** (derived from the Sanskrit words *Mool* meaning root or origin, and *Ank* meaning number) represents your core elemental nature. It is also popularly known as your **Driver Number** or **Birth Number**.

While external circumstances and environments shift throughout life, traditional teachings suggest that your Mulank highlights your innate temperament, instinctive reactions, and baseline psychological disposition.

### How to Calculate Your Mulank

Calculating your Mulank is remarkably straightforward: take the calendar day of the month on which you were born and sum its digits until you arrive at a single number between **1 and 9**:

- **Days 1 to 9:** Your Mulank is simply the birth day itself ($1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, \text{ or } 9$).
- **Days 10 to 19:** Add the two digits:
  - $10 \rightarrow 1 + 0 = \mathbf{1}$
  - $14 \rightarrow 1 + 4 = \mathbf{5}$
  - $19 \rightarrow 1 + 9 = 10 \rightarrow 1 + 0 = \mathbf{1}$
- **Days 20 to 29:** Add the two digits:
  - $22 \rightarrow 2 + 2 = \mathbf{4}$
  - $28 \rightarrow 2 + 8 = 10 \rightarrow 1 + 0 = \mathbf{1}$
- **Days 30 & 31:**
  - $30 \rightarrow 3 + 0 = \mathbf{3}$
  - $31 \rightarrow 3 + 1 = \mathbf{4}$
