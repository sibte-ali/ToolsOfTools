## Celebrate Your Bond with the Friendship Calculator

True friendship is one of the most rewarding treasures in life. Whether you want to celebrate your lifelong childhood best friend, tease a new roommate, or check how well your gaming squad aligns, our **friendship calculator by name** gives you a fun, shareable score in seconds.

Built with an entirely independent mathematical model from our love calculator, this tool focuses squarely on platonic loyalty, communicative ease, and shared humor.

### How the Friendship Algorithm Operates

To ensure fair and repeatable fun, our calculator follows a four-step deterministic method:

1. **Alphabetical Symmetry:** Both names are sanitized and sorted alphabetically. "Chris & Jordan" yields the identical score as "Jordan & Chris".
2. **FNV-1a 32-bit Hashing:** The combined string is processed through the Fowler-Noll-Vo algorithm, which provides exceptional dispersion across character combinations.
3. **Harmonic Letter Weighting:** The algorithm analyzes overlapping characters and vowel distributions to compute a subtle harmony bonus.
4. **Camaraderie Scaling:** The final integer maps into a healthy, uplifting range between **45% and 98%**, avoiding awkward zero scores while highlighting unique relational strengths.
